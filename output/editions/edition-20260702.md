RESEARCH

IEEE Spectrum reports that Princeton researchers are using reinforcement learning and diffusion models to automate the design of radio-frequency integrated circuits, generating novel RF layouts that achieve record performance and drastically reduce design time for wireless technologies including 5G and satellite communications.

https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-radio-chip-design

Researchers on arXiv present a capacity-controlled framework for multi-view stylization of 3D Gaussian Splatting scenes, addressing the cross-view inconsistency problems that plague existing 3D stylization methods. The approach replaces per-view 2D feature-matching losses with a more stable allocation scheme aimed at coherent stylistic transfer across viewpoints.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26754

Researchers on arXiv present SatSplatDiff, a geometry-preserving generative refinement method for satellite-based Gaussian Splatting that uses pretrained generative priors to fill in building facades poorly supervised by top-down satellite imagery. The approach targets surface holes and degraded fidelity in large-scale satellite 3D reconstruction.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27223

An arXiv paper fine-tunes a pretrained 3D-aware face GAN using reinforcement learning from human feedback applied directly to NeRF radiance-field density values, sidestepping the mesh conversion step common in prior 3D RLHF pipelines. The method aims to align generated face geometry with human aesthetic preferences without surface-supervised training data.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27305

A new arXiv paper, Look-Before-Move, proposes a framework for camera planning in dynamic 3D story worlds in which an embodied agent actively decides what visual evidence to acquire before moving, advancing visual perception beyond passive interpretation toward narrative-grounded attention for world models.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26964

An updated arXiv paper proposes a collaborative multi-agent framework for zero-shot 3D scene understanding that assigns a Planning Agent to handle high-level visual reasoning, aiming to overcome the perspective and perception bottlenecks faced by prior video-keyframe approaches using multimodal large language models.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24649

An arXiv paper, GaussLite: Online Task-Conditioned 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Robotic Mapping, introduces a task-driven 3D Gaussian Splatting system that concentrates compute on scene regions relevant to the robot's task, aiming to enable more efficient real-time mapping onboard embodied agents.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30809

According to arXiv, Knowledge-Driven Dimension Estimation from a Single Image proposes a 3D asset generation technique for digital-twin construction that uses prior knowledge to estimate real-world scale from a single image, targeting simulation pipelines used to validate in-vehicle camera systems against traffic signs and other high-mounted objects.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30896

Researchers publishing on arXiv propose MemLearner, a learned context memory retrieval method for video world models that aims to maintain scene consistency over extended durations, addressing failures of rule-based frame retrieval under occlusions and dynamic objects.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31734

A new arXiv paper explores using Large Language Models to drive decision-making for virtual humans in emergency simulation scenarios, aiming to produce agents that exhibit spatial awareness and competent behavior when interacting with their environments. The authors argue that LLM-driven personalities can improve the believability and agency of simulated characters in training and simulation contexts.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31038

A new arXiv paper introduces Contour-Adaptive Mixed Environment Overlays (CAMEO), a mixed reality interface technique that drapes digital surfaces along the contours of a user's physical environment rather than floating windows in a cockpit-style layout, aiming to reduce occlusion and preserve access to real-world objects in MR workspaces.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00518
