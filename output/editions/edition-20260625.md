Researchers on arXiv present TurboGS, an error-guided training framework that accelerates 3D Gaussian Splatting by concentrating optimization on perceptually informative pixels through sparse sampling. The method targets consumer-level applications that need fast 3DGS optimization with high-fidelity novel view rendering without sacrificing fine details.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15924

An arXiv paper introduces VEPHand, an end-to-end pipeline for dynamic hand performance capture and registration designed for view-efficient multi-view setups using roughly 20 cameras. The work addresses geometric ambiguities in 3D hand reconstruction that have limited photometric capture systems used in digital human creation.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15966

Researchers on arXiv describe LentiAvatar, a Gaussian head-avatar system that pairs monocular portrait video capture with subpixel-encoded glasses-free lenticular displays to enable real-time autostereoscopic telepresence. The approach removes the need for specialized capture rigs that have historically constrained stereoscopic video communication.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10550

A paper on arXiv presents HairLRM, a strand-based hair modeling approach using large reconstruction models that introduces structural constraints to resolve global occlusion and local directionality failures in 3D hair geometry inferred from 2D imagery.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15238

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17783

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17427
