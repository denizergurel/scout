RESEARCH

An arXiv study compares head, gaze, and finger-based object selection techniques in augmented reality for people with low vision, evaluating their ability and preferences given potential visual and gaze control challenges. The work aims to inform accessible selection-based AR augmentation that reduces distraction and visual clutter.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06778

Researchers introduce SPEAR, a Python-based simulator for photorealistic embodied AI research that can programmatically control any Unreal Engine application, according to an arXiv paper. The system targets limitations in generality, programmability, and rendering speed found in existing photorealistic simulators used to train embodied agents and generate synthetic visual data.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06701

Researchers on arXiv introduce WildCity, a real-world multimodal city-scale testbed spanning tens of square kilometers for rendering, simulation, and spatial intelligence benchmarks, aimed at pushing scene reconstruction and embodied AI foundation models beyond current single-scene limits.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06838

A paper posted to arXiv introduces EmbodiedGen V2, a generative 3D world engine designed to build executable, simulation-ready environments for embodied AI. The authors argue that while sim-ready 3D asset generation has advanced quickly, assembling assets into policy-ready task environments remains largely manual, and they propose a unified representation that connects cross-simulator assets and interaction affordances to enable more scalable closed-loop learning.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07459
