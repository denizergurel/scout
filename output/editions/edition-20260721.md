RESEARCH

Researchers on arXiv present the first implementation of a 3D Gaussian splatting renderer on an Intelligence Processing Unit, using 1,472 independent tiles with only on-chip SRAM to render real-world Gaussian scenes — a step toward efficient sensor-processor architectures relevant to XR rendering.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15951

Researchers on arXiv propose a new readout method for video pretrained backbones that improves detection of AI-generated videos by better capturing the subtle inter-frame temporal artifacts that distinguish synthetic footage from real recordings.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15321

In a paper posted to arXiv, researchers introduce GS-RealBlur, a data acquisition framework for real-world image deblurring that uses a handheld camera to capture paired blurry and sharp images, addressing the trade-off between the unrealistic quality of synthetic blur and the inflexibility of complex multi-camera capture rigs.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15401

A revised arXiv paper argues that generative models contain implicit 3D spatial priors that can be unleashed to improve scene understanding in multimodal large language models, offering an alternative to explicit 3D modalities and geometric scaffolding that the authors say are limited by data scarcity and generalization challenges.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19235

Researchers publishing on arXiv describe MotionForesight, a system that repurposes video generation models to predict future 3D scene flow — the likely trajectories of points on manipulable objects — from short monocular video clips of human-object interaction. The work aims to give robots and embodied agents a way to anticipate physical consequences of interactions from ordinary video, rather than requiring depth sensors or explicit 3D supervision.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16192

An arXiv paper proposes Video Streaming Thinking, a paradigm that lets online video language models perform synchronized logical reasoning while continuing to perceive incoming video frames, addressing the latency penalty that test-time scaling methods typically impose on real-time video understanding. The authors position the approach as a step toward responsive video assistants that can watch and think simultaneously rather than pausing to reason.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12262

Xiaomi researchers published Xiaomi-Robotics-1, a foundational vision-language-action model trained on more than 100,000 hours of real-world trajectories, described in an arXiv paper as capable of following diverse language instructions for mobile manipulation in unseen environments and adapting to novel tasks with minimal fine-tuning.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15330

An arXiv paper proposes a standardized benchmark for skeleton-based rehabilitation assessment using deep learning, applying video-based skeleton extraction to detect subtle deviations from ideal motion and enable objective evaluation of patient performance in clinical rehabilitation settings.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21018

An arXiv paper on Visual Language Navigation presents instance-enriched semantic maps that combine object-level detail with large language model reasoning, aiming to improve the robustness of embodied agents following natural language instructions in complex indoor environments.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12630
